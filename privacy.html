Privacy Policy.

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Draft notice: This is a draft policy for the project website and planned desktop app. Before public release or TikTok review, the operator must verify that every statement matches the actual software, hosting, data flows, retention and contact arrangements.

1. Scope

This Policy describes the intended privacy approach for the NAREZ 3.0 website and desktop application. The website is an informational project page. The desktop application is under development; features and data practices may change before release.

2. Information the app may process

Depending on the features you choose to use, a released version of NAREZ may process:

  • Local project files and media that you select for editing;
  • Application settings, project history and export preferences;
  • Text and timestamps generated for subtitles or clip suggestions;
  • Account identifiers and analytics data returned by a platform after you authorize a connection;
  • Authentication tokens needed to maintain an authorized connection.

Do not assume every listed feature is currently active. The released app must explain which information is processed, where it is stored and how long it is retained.

3. Platform connections

If TikTok or another platform connection becomes available, authorization should use the platform's official authorization flow. NAREZ should request only the permissions needed for the enabled features. The data accessible to NAREZ depends on platform approval, granted scopes, account eligibility and API availability. Connecting an account does not guarantee access to every metric or historical record.

Third-party platforms process information under their own privacy policies. Review the relevant platform policy before connecting an account.

4. How information is used

Information processed by NAREZ is intended to provide the features you request, display project and analytics information, save your preferences, troubleshoot errors and protect the application. NAREZ should not sell personal information or use connected-platform data for unrelated purposes.

5. Storage, security and retention

The final app must disclose whether each category of information is stored locally on your device or transmitted to a server or service provider. Authentication credentials and tokens must be protected using appropriate security controls. Information should be retained only for as long as needed for the feature or purpose, then deleted or anonymized where feasible. The exact implementation and retention periods must be confirmed before release.

6. Sharing

NAREZ does not intend to sell personal information. Information may be processed by a platform you choose to connect, or by infrastructure providers required to operate a feature, subject to the final product's disclosed data flows. The app should not share your media or account data with other parties without a clear feature purpose or your instruction, except where required by law.

7. Your choices and deletion

You should be able to choose whether to connect a third-party account and to disconnect it. Disconnecting should stop future access through that authorization; it may not delete information already held by the third-party platform. Local project files and app data can be removed using the controls or operating-system tools provided by the final release. The final app must document how to request deletion of any server-side data, if such data is collected.

8. Children

NAREZ is not intended for children who are not legally permitted to use the software or connected services in their jurisdiction. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact the project through the support link below.

9. Changes and contact

This Policy may be updated as the project develops. The latest version will be published here. For privacy questions or requests, contact the project through the NAREZ GitHub issue tracker. Do not post passwords, access tokens or other secrets in public issues.
